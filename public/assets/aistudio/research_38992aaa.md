## 2. Business identity and relationship

The restaurant is listed as **Katha by Bhupa Greens**. A Google-style business result identifies it as a restaurant/cafe/family restaurant at **Bhupa Greens, NH27, Shahpur, Darbhanga, Bastauli, Bihar 846005**.

The Menucard listing also places Katha inside the Bhupa Greens campus on NH57 in Shahpur, Darbhanga. Tripadvisor describes the location as NH27, Bhupa Greens, opposite Swami Vivekanand Hospital, Darbhanga 846005.

This supports the client's instruction that Katha is located within Bhupa Greens. The website should describe it as **“Katha by Bhupa Greens, located inside the Bhupa Greens campus”** rather than presenting the restaurant as a separate unrelated property.

## 5. Google rating and review-count discrepancy

The project brief previously supplied a figure of **4.7 stars and 170+ reviews** for the Google Maps trust section.

The current structured business result retrieved on 24 November 2026 shows **4.5 stars and 166 ratings** for Katha by Bhupa Greens.

These figures do not match. Review counts and ratings can change, and search tools may refresh at different times. Do not hard-code either figure until the exact listing is checked live by the owner.

Recommended website treatment:
- Prefer a live Places API value for rating and rating count, if API access and budget are approved.
- Otherwise show a button such as “Read our Google reviews” that opens the exact listing.
- Do not write fake reviews or claim the 4.7/170+ figure until verified.

- Soups and salads.
- Indian food.
- South Indian food.
- Korean items.
- Biryani.
- Noodles.

A 16-page PDF menu was available through the third-party MenuCard listing at the time of research. The first pages show examples such as:
- Tea and coffee: Masala Chai, Ginger Chai, Butter Chai, Filter Coffee, Pheteli Coffee, Cold Coffee and Hot Chocolate.
- Indian breads: Tandoori Roti, Naan, Lachha Paratha and Khamiri Roti.
- Rice: Plain Rice, Ghee Rice, Jeera Rice, Peas Pulao, Chicken Berry Pulao, Imli Rice and Lemon Rice.
- Starters: Paneer Tikka, Paneer Malai Tikka, Chicken Seekh Kebab, Chicken Tikka, Chicken Malai Tikka and Tandoori Chicken.
- Main courses: Dal Makhani, Panchmel Dal, Dal Tadka, Paneer Butter Masala, Paneer Kadai, Veg Diwani Handi and mushroom dishes.
- Biryani: Hyderabad-style chicken and veg biryani, plus egg and aloo dum biryani.
- South Indian: Dosa and Uttapam variations.
- Other items: Korean burgers, Korean fried chicken, fries, pao, bun maska, salads, subs/sandwiches.

The public PDF may be old or incomplete. Treat dish examples as menu research, not a guarantee of current availability. Confirm the current menu, spelling, category structure, dietary labels and prices with the restaurant. Do not copy the PDF's displayed prices into the new website without approval.
