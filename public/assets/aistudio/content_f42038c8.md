## 12. Copy to avoid

Do not use claims such as “the best restaurant in Bihar,” “unforgettable culinary journey,” “world-class dining,” “premium experience,” “freshest ingredients every day,” “authentic recipes passed down generations,” or “award-winning” unless the owner can support them with clear facts.

Do not promise delivery, table availability, a particular opening time or a dish's availability unless the restaurant confirms it.
