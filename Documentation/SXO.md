# SXO – Search Experience Optimisation

The website should make it easy for someone to decide whether to visit Katha and find the restaurant once they are ready.

## 1. First-screen clarity

A visitor should quickly see:
- Katha is a restaurant.
- It is inside Bhupa Greens.
- The menu can be viewed online.
- The location and contact links are available.

## 2. Menu experience

- Organise the menu by category.
- Keep item names and prices readable.
- Clearly identify vegetarian/non-vegetarian choices only when confirmed.
- Mark unavailable items rather than leaving stale items visible.
- Keep the menu responsive and accessible.
- Do not force users to download a PDF to read the menu.

## 3. Trust

- Show real food and restaurant photos.
- Provide a direct Google Maps link.
- Show current Google rating/count only if verified or fetched through an approved API.
- Link to the original review source.
- Do not display copied or invented reviews.

## 4. Conversion

Provide simple actions:
- View Menu.
- Call.
- WhatsApp, if approved.
- Get Directions.
- Send an enquiry.

Do not make a contact form longer than needed. Make it clear that a message is an enquiry, not a confirmed table reservation.

## 5. Accessibility and mobile

Use semantic headings, keyboard-accessible menus, visible focus, labelled fields, clear error messages, good colour contrast, readable font sizes and reduced-motion support.

## 6. Performance

Aim for Core Web Vitals:
- LCP: 2.5 seconds or less.
- INP: 200 ms or less.
- CLS: 0.1 or less.

These are targets; measure field performance after launch. Compress images, use responsive sizes and avoid loading unnecessary third-party scripts.

## 7. Measurement

Track menu opens, category views, call clicks, WhatsApp clicks, directions clicks, blog visits and form submissions. Do not send names, phone numbers or enquiry text to analytics.
