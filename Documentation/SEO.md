# SEO Plan – Katha by Bhupa Greens

## 1. Search intent

Core local search themes to validate over time:
- Katha by Bhupa Greens
- restaurant in Darbhanga
- cafe in Darbhanga
- restaurant near NH27 Darbhanga
- restaurant inside Bhupa Greens
- food near Mabbi / Shahpur Darbhanga
- biryani in Darbhanga
- South Indian food in Darbhanga
- Katha menu
- Katha by Bhupa Greens contact and directions

Do not treat these as search-volume facts. Use Search Console data after launch.

## 2. Page-level SEO

Each page needs:
- Unique title and meta description.
- One main H1.
- Clear subheadings.
- Descriptive, useful copy.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph title, description and image.
- Internal links to menu, contact and relevant articles.
- Descriptive image filenames and alt text.

Suggested URLs:
- `/`
- `/about`
- `/menu`
- `/menu/[category]` only if category pages add real value.
- `/journal`
- `/journal/[slug]`
- `/contact`

## 3. Local SEO

- Confirm the exact Google Business Profile for Katha, not only Bhupa Greens.
- Keep restaurant name, phone, address, hours and website consistent.
- Link to the Katha Maps pin, not the parent banquet listing.
- Add original food and restaurant photographs.
- Keep Instagram and Facebook links current.
- Encourage genuine guest reviews without incentives or pressure.
- Respond to reviews politely and do not disclose customer information.

## 4. Structured data

Use `Restaurant` or the most accurate supported local-business type with verified fields:
- `name`
- `url`
- `telephone`
- `email`
- `address`
- `geo`
- `openingHoursSpecification`
- `servesCuisine` only for confirmed cuisines
- `menu`
- `sameAs`
- `image`

Use `Menu`, `MenuSection` and `MenuItem` only when the menu is visible and current.

Important:
- Do not invent rating or review count.
- Do not add Google aggregate rating to a self-serving LocalBusiness schema expecting Google review stars. Google's documentation says local businesses reviewing themselves are not eligible for the review-star feature.
- Do not mark up content that is not visible to users.

## 5. Technical SEO

- Generate XML sitemap.
- Configure robots.txt correctly.
- Keep staging/preview deployments out of the index.
- Use HTTPS and one canonical domain.
- Return correct 404/410 status codes.
- Optimise images and prevent layout shift.
- Add breadcrumbs where useful.
- Monitor Search Console and Core Web Vitals.

## 6. Blog SEO

Each article should have:
- One clear question or topic.
- A useful, specific title.
- A short introduction.
- Descriptive subheadings.
- Real restaurant/menu context.
- Internal links to the menu and contact page.
- Original imagery where available.
- Author and publish date.
- SEO title and meta description.

Avoid mass-producing thin location pages or keyword-stuffed blog posts.
