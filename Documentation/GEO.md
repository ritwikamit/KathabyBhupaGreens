# GEO – Generative Engine Optimisation

GEO aims to make accurate information about Katha easier for generative search systems to find and interpret. There is no guaranteed way to secure a citation or recommendation.

## 1. Maintain one source of truth

The website should provide the clearest current information about the restaurant's identity, location, contact details, menu and hours. Keep the Google Business Profile and official social profiles aligned with it.

## 2. Publish useful factual pages

- A clear About page explaining Katha's location inside Bhupa Greens.
- A menu page with real categories and current items.
- A contact page with the correct Maps pin.
- Blog articles with specific restaurant/menu information.
- FAQs answering actual visitor questions.

## 3. Build local credibility

- Keep the correct Katha Google Business Profile complete.
- Use original photos.
- Encourage honest guest reviews.
- Keep phone, address and hours consistent across credible directories.
- Participate in genuine local references and food listings.
- Do not buy spam links or fabricate reviews.

## 4. Technical discoverability

- Allow legitimate crawlers to access public pages.
- Keep sitemap and canonical URLs correct.
- Render important text in crawlable HTML.
- Link blog posts to menu and contact pages.
- Use structured data that matches visible content.

## 5. Avoid

- Fake testimonials.
- Unsupported “best in Darbhanga” claims.
- Hidden keyword text.
- Duplicate AI-generated articles.
- Promises of guaranteed placement in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini or other answer systems.
