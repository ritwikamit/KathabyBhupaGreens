# Katha Journal – Blog Content Plan

## Purpose

The blog should answer questions people may have about the food, menu and restaurant. It should also give Katha a place to share genuine updates. Articles should be useful, based on the current menu and checked by the restaurant team.

## Suggested categories

1. Menu Notes
2. Food & Ingredients
3. Seasonal Picks
4. Restaurant Updates
5. Visit Katha

## Article ideas

| Working title | What it should cover | Verify before writing |
|---|---|---|
| A Look at the Katha Menu | Current menu categories and how to browse them | Menu is current |
| Biryani Options at Katha | Biryani types actually available | Recipes, serving sizes and availability |
| Tea, Coffee and a Break at Katha | Drinks listed on the current menu | Current beverage list |
| South Indian Choices on the Menu | Dosa and uttapam options | Current availability |
| A Few Korean-Style Bites to Try | Korean-inspired items listed on menu | Current dishes and exact names |
| Desserts at Katha | Sweets and ice cream options | Seasonal availability |
| Finding Katha Inside Bhupa Greens | Directions from the campus entrance | Exact guest access route |
| Planning a Group Visit | How to contact the restaurant about groups | Reservation/group policy |

## Article structure

- A clear title.
- A short introduction that answers the topic.
- Two to four useful subheadings.
- Specific food or location details.
- A real photo if available.
- A relevant link to the menu or contact page.
- Author and publish date.

## Editorial rules

- Do not invent quotes, chef stories or customer reviews.
- Avoid generic food-history paragraphs that do not relate to Katha.
- Do not publish old prices as current.
- Use simple language and short sentences.
- Have the restaurant confirm dish names, ingredients and availability.
- Review old articles when the menu changes.
