# Design Consistency Document – Katha by Bhupa Greens

## 1. Brand assets

- Main restaurant mark: `assets/katha-by-bhupa-greens-logo-transparent.png`.
- Agency mark: `assets/ac-custom-labs-logo-transparent.png`.
- Do not recreate, recolour or distort the logos without approval.
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space around each logo.
- Use the restaurant logo in the header and footer. Use the agency logo only in the footer credit.

## 2. Brand colour direction

The supplied Katha logo uses a strong magenta wordmark and dark supporting script. Use the logo as the colour reference.

Suggested working tokens:
- Katha magenta: `#D92378` (sampled visual approximation; confirm from master brand file).
- Ink/charcoal: `#29252A`.
- Warm ivory: `#FFF9F4`.
- Soft blush: `#F8E8EF`.
- Neutral grey: `#756D73`.
- White: `#FFFFFF`.

Use magenta for key actions and selected details, not for every heading or paragraph. Keep body text dark for readability. Check final colour contrast.

## 3. Typography

Suggested pairing:
- Display/headings: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display.
- Body/UI: Manrope or Inter.
- The supplied logo lettering remains part of the image; do not approximate it with a web font.

Use a consistent type scale:
- H1: 48–64 px desktop, 36–42 px mobile.
- H2: 32–40 px desktop, 28–32 px mobile.
- H3: 22–26 px.
- Body: 16–18 px with comfortable line height.
- Small labels: 12–14 px, not used for essential information.

## 4. Layout

- Keep a clear grid and generous spacing.
- Keep the top navigation compact.
- Use real food and restaurant photographs.
- Use consistent corner radii and image treatments.
- Avoid placing too many cards or badges next to each other.
- Avoid a template-like stack of identical sections.
- Ensure the menu remains readable and easy to scan.

## 5. Photography

Priority assets:
- Restaurant entrance and sign.
- Dining interior and seating.
- Close-up food photography of actual menu items.
- Tea, coffee and dessert photographs.
- Group/table setting photos where guests have consented.
- Exterior/campus arrival context to help visitors find Katha.

Do not use stock photographs to represent actual menu dishes. Add accurate alt text.

## 6. Motion

- Use restrained fades and small transitions.
- Avoid scroll-jacking, constant parallax or long page entrances.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not autoplay audio.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold gallery images.

## 7. Logo placement and footer credit

Header:
- Katha logo on a light or warm-ivory background.
- Maintain sufficient logo width so the “by Bhupa Greens” line remains legible.

Footer:
- Use a dark charcoal or deep plum footer strip so the white/red ACCustomLabs wordmark has contrast.
- Wording: “Designed and developed by” followed by the ACCustomLabs logo.
- Link the agency logo to the official ACCustomLabs website after its final URL is confirmed.
- Do not use the agency logo as a restaurant brand mark.

## 8. Responsive checks

Test:
- 320 px, 375 px, 390 px and 430 px mobile widths.
- Tablet portrait and landscape.
- Desktop and wide desktop.
- Menu filter controls and long item names.
- Blog text and image sizing.
- Footer logo and social links.
